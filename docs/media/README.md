## Series: Inside Out, Eye to Galaxy, Galactic Helix, To the Edge

Four films, 2026, played in this order. Artwork by NMAI Research, made with Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) in Claude Code: the frames are rendered by Python code that Claude wrote at the author's direction. The web copies are H.264 re-encodes of the colour masters without audio or container metadata; each poster is a frame from its film. Shapes and colours are illustrative, and scale is compressed where the films say so.

- Inside Out: from a speculative quantum foam through the gluon sea, quarks, atoms, DNA and cells to a human eye. On-screen figures cite CODATA (Planck length) and the CMS Collaboration (collider reach).
- Eye to Galaxy: from the eye through a face, crowds, Manchester's streets, the night-time lights of North-West England, Great Britain and Europe to the Earth and the solar system. Street map © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (openstreetmap.org/copyright). Night lights: NASA Earth Observatory. Coastlines: Natural Earth.
- Galactic Helix: the solar system's path through the Milky Way.
- To the Edge: the Milky Way, the Local Group, the galaxies around Laniakea, a simulated cosmic web and the microwave sky, looping back to the opening. Galaxy positions: McConnachie 2012, Astron. J. 144, 4, and the 2MASS Redshift Survey, Huchra et al. 2012, Astrophys. J. Suppl. Ser. 199, 26, both via the VizieR catalogue access tool, CDS, Strasbourg Astronomical Observatory, France (DOI 10.26093/cds/vizier). The cosmic web is a Zel'dovich-approximation simulation computed for the film, not observed data. Microwave sky: WMAP nine-year data, image credit NASA / WMAP Science Team.
