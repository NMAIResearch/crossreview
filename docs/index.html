How it works

A little structure. Another perspective.
  1. 01

    Bring your work.

    Submit a public repo, its claims, the AI tools used and the problems you already know about.

  2. 02

    Check the basics.

    An automatic pre-check reads it without running it: README, licence, leaked keys, broken files.

  3. 03

    Look a little closer.

    Choose a project in your domain if you can. Other perspectives are welcome. Mark it as picked up, then review each claim with quotes or file and line.

  4. 04

    Keep it moving.

    Your first submission is free. After that, give one review per submission.
