The exchange

Review for review.

Think your project works, or could work, but have no one to ask? Swap reviews of work made with AI: give one, get one.

Crossreview is for AI builders and reviewers who are willing to ask for help, receive critique and exchange solutions. The aim is useful work grounded in evidence, with less sycophancy and more honest critique.

Bring a research protocol, an analysis, a tool, a design or another project made with AI. Say what you want checked. A review is another person's assessment, not a guarantee of correctness or safety. Submitting does not guarantee a reviewer.

You do not have to be an expert. If you have experience in a domain, share it. A mathematician may need a lawyer's perspective, a chess player a visual designer, or an astrophysicist a medical professional. Say what you can assess and where your knowledge ends.

What you need may already exist. Ask your agent to search GitHub and Hugging Face for relevant tools before building something new. Look for an existing solution ↗

How it works

A little structure. Another perspective.
  1. 01

    Bring your work.

    Submit a public repo, its claims, the AI tools used and the problems you already know about.

  2. 02

    Check the basics.

    An automatic pre-check reads the repo without running submitted code. It checks basic files and selected credential patterns; it does not establish correctness or security.

  3. 03

    Look a little closer.

    Choose a project in your domain if you can. Other perspectives are welcome. Mark it as picked up, then review each claim with quotes or file and line.

  4. 04

    Keep it moving.

    Your first submission is free. After that, give one review per submission.

Two roles

Read the rubric ↗
↗

Sight, the author

Explain the project's purpose, what you want checked and the problems you already know about. You decide what to change after reviewing the findings.

↙

Severity, the reviewer

Match the review to the work: a protocol needs its method checked; a tool needs its behaviour checked. Report evidence and limits. If AI helps, use a different vendor from the maker; that alone does not establish independence.

The review board

Open the exchange

See who has picked up a project before joining in. Reviewers may share their domain experience to help readers weigh their perspective. It is optional and self-described; the evidence still matters.
